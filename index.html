01 / VERIFIED SAMPLES

46 files. A limited sample.

46 files from software the developer has published are monitored through VirusTotal. This is a record of false positives and detection burden on this sample, from the developer’s perspective; it does not represent any engine’s overall performance. Policy-based detections such as PUA and Riskware are also included.

02 / SAME CONDITIONS

Longer detections, higher scores

Score = total detection duration across files divided by 24 hours. One file flagged for a day earns one point. Ongoing cases count up to the cutoff. Monthly scores count only duration falling within that month.

03 / ACCOUNTABILITY

No response does not mean cleared

Duration runs from the first observed detection to the first observation confirming a clean result. Actual onset and clearance times may differ. An engine not responding is not treated as clearance; cases with unconfirmed clearance keep accumulating in the provisional score. Files excluded from monitoring (replaced by a new version or no longer distributed) stop counting at their last scan.

The wording on this page expresses an opinion about an evaluation structure centered on detection counts. It does not assert the intent of any specific vendor. All figures are based on observation records. Request a correction ↗

The antivirus: one line of warning.
The developer: dozens of lines of explanation.

Users stop downloading. Software gets deleted. The developer is suspected of writing malware. The cost of a false positive falls more on whoever made the software than on whoever raised the alarm.

This page does not evaluate the overall detection performance of any antivirus. It publicly documents misjudgments of legitimate software and the responses that follow, to reduce the harm to independent developers.
